Weather: Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD), https://opendata.dwd.de/weather/ . CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Values are converted to displayed units; hourly values are grouped into daily summaries, and radar pixels are coloured and projected. Missing readings are omitted or hatched. Source terms: https://www.dwd.de/copyright .

City search: Open-Meteo, https://open-meteo.com/ , and GeoNames, https://www.geonames.org/ . Place metadata: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Compact labels are assembled from returned names/regions/countries; user-edited labels are marked. Free API for non-commercial use only, subject to quotas: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms . Licence: https://open-meteo.com/en/licence . Only explicit search text is submitted; memory cache can be cleared in Places. Optional foreground device location resolves to stations locally without reverse geocoding. Android providers may use network services. Selected positions and favourites are stored as private snapshots, not location history.

Hourly precipitation probability is DWD R101 (>0.1 mm in the preceding hour); gusts are DWD FX1 (preceding-hour maximum), converted to km/h. Missing values remain unavailable. Sunrise/sunset are calculated locally using NOAA/Meeus-style solar mathematics and a standard -0.833 degree horizon; no astronomy service receives your coordinates. Times are estimates without terrain/building/cloud obstruction: https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/calcdetails.html .

Basemap and geographical timezone data © OpenStreetMap contributors: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Timezone boundary database: timezone-boundary-builder, https://github.com/evansiroky/timezone-boundary-builder . Database licence: Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0, https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ . The extracted database source is distributed in this repository: https://codeberg.org/k4r/drizziq .

Timezone lookup: The Dark Sky Company, LLC and PhotoStructure, CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Time zones are approximate at borders and can be overridden.
